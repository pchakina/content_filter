Memorial Health System — Provider Credentialing Policy (Sample, fictitious, for demo use only)

1. Scope
This policy applies to all physicians and allied health practitioners seeking initial
credentialing or recredentialing to practice at any Memorial Health System facility.

2. Verification Requirements
All practitioners must have current, unrestricted state medical licensure in every
state where they will practice. Expired licenses in any practicing state must be
renewed before privileges can be granted in that state.

3. Board Certification
Board certification is preferred but not mandatory for initial credentialing, except
for practitioners applying for privileges in a subspecialty (e.g., Infectious Disease),
where active certification in that subspecialty is required. A lapsed subspecialty
certification with a renewal in process may be granted a 90-day conditional window.

4. Background and Sanctions Checks
Every applicant must clear an OIG exclusion check, a SAM.gov debarment check, an NPDB
query, and a multi-state criminal background check with no unresolved findings.
Malpractice claims history is reviewed but a closed claim with no judgment does not
by itself disqualify an applicant.

5. Recredentialing Cycle
Recredentialing occurs every 24 months, or sooner if a license, certification, or
background check flag changes status.

6. Escalation
Any application with an expired license, a lapsed required certification, or an open
sanction must be escalated to the Credentialing Committee for manual review before
a final determination is issued.
